## 📸 Overview

**[Game Name]** blends the relaxing vibe of classic fishing games with high-intensity arcade bullet hell mechanics. Cast your line into serene waters, but be prepared—once a fish takes the bait, you'll have to survive its unique attack patterns to reel it in.

Sell your terrifying deep-sea catches, upgrade your gear, and venture into dangerous waters for even bigger bounties.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **🔲 Dodge to Reel:** Combat takes place in a compact battle box. Evade dynamic bullet patterns tailored to each fish's personality and difficulty.
* **🎣 Gear Progression:** 
  * **Rods & Lines:** Increase your defense, survive hits longer, and drain fish stamina faster.
  * **Bait & Lures:** Attract rarer, more lucrative—and infinitely more aggressive—monsters.
* **💰 Risk & Reward:** Cheaper bait nets calm fish for steady income; high-tier bait pulls up screen-clearing abyssal leviathans that pay out fortune or defeat.
* **🎨 Retro Aesthetic:** Pixel art paired with a dual-tone chiptune soundtrack (lo-fi while fishing, frantic during battle).
